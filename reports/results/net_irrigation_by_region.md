# Net irrigation requirement by region (Phase 8.1)

8 crops x 4 IMD subdivisions (+ the area-weighted state-wide "maharashtra" figure) x 2 rainfall scenarios (normal = the 30-year average of each year's monthly water balance; dry = the real observed year nearest the 20th percentile of usable rain, listed per region below). Net irrigation is the SUM OF MONTHLY DEFICITS over the crop's season (a surplus month does not cover a later deficit); `Peff normal` is the season's total effective rain, `rain used` the part the crop can actually use. See docs/water.md section 8 for methodology and citations.
