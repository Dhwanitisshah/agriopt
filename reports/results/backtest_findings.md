**Statistical caveat (stated plainly, not buried)**: only four years (2016-2019) have a realized outcome. With n=4 no sign or Wilcoxon test can reach p<0.125, so none is reported; backtest_summary.md lists the per-year deltas instead. Treat the results as four case studies, not as evidence that AgriOpt beats (or fails to beat) B1 in any statistically meaningful sense.
