# Findings v2: risk objective, NSGA-III, reference grid (Phase 5)

- **B2 (profit-max) concentrates into 2 crop(s) (vs B1's 8) and carries less portfolio risk than B1** (Rs 86,304 vs Rs 122,519) -- profit-maximizing concentration is not riskier here, in the sense of this covariance-based portfolio metric.
- **Model B (risk-aware, NSGA-III) vs Model A's recommendation, full trade**: profit +1.6% (Rs 291,925 -> Rs 296,565), water +1.3% (m3 52,242 -> 52,936), fert +15.4% (kg 705 -> 813), portfolio risk -4.2% (Rs 63,910 -> Rs 61,218). Model B is not a free lunch on every axis -- report the full vector, not just the objectives it improved.
- B1 (current mix)'s worst historical year would have earned Rs -10,888 profit (year 2015, out of 18 years evaluated), 10th percentile Rs 40,740, 1 loss year(s) -- read alongside portfolio_risk, since the discrete historical record captures downside shape (e.g. skew, a single very bad year) that a single sqrt(x'Sigma x) number does not.
- The v2 grid reference front mostly resolves Phase 3's counter-intuitive finding that NSGA-II's hypervolume exceeded the exact LP reference's in every scenario (mean ratio was 1.15-1.36x vs v1) -- against the denser v2 grid, the mean ratio drops to 1.005x, and 3/6 market-price scenarios now show NSGA-II at or below the reference. The remaining 3/6 scenarios (all msp_floor) still edge slightly above 1.0 (up to ~1.02x, see optim_front_quality_v2.md) -- MSP flooring narrows the profit range enough that even the denser grid may not fully cover the true surface there, so a small residual gap is plausible rather than a red flag.
- Sugarcane's revenue CV (0.10) is far below the other crops' mean (0.50) because its price is a constant FRP, not a mandi series -- its low measured risk is an artifact of missing price variance, not genuinely lower agronomic risk; flagged, not treated as a free lunch.
- The revenue covariance matrix (Sigma) came out PSD without needing the nearest-PSD clip in this run (min eigenvalue before clipping: 5,869,553) -- expected, since it is a Hadamard product of a real (if pairwise-incomplete) sample covariance matrix with a rank-1 PSD scaling matrix (R R'), which is PSD by the Schur product theorem whenever the underlying covariance itself is; the clip exists as a safety net for cases where pairwise-incomplete-years covariance breaks that.
- Crop_Year's harvest-window convention (kharif = Oct-Dec of year t, rabi = Mar-May of year t+1) is an ASSUMPTION, not verified against an independent Maharashtra harvest calendar (see agriopt.optim.risk module docstring) -- if wrong, Rabi crops' revenue years could be off by one, which would mostly affect cross-crop correlation estimates between Kharif and Rabi crops, not the overall risk magnitudes.
- **Sugarcane risk sensitivity: Model B's recommendation is NOT robust to the FRP-price-artifact assumption** -- replacing sugarcane's measured std (0.095) with the median of the other 7 crops' std (0.273, correlations unchanged) moves sugarcane hectares from 1.68 to 0.44 ha (73.7% shift). See risk_strategies.md 'Sensitivity: sugarcane risk' for the full before/after table.
